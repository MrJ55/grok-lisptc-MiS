## Updated decisions

- Keep the full `docs/` corpus as canonical Git research provenance.
- Promote operational OSS/DMN knowledge into MiS as typed, versioned, source-linked protocol objects and approved procedures.
- Use Vestige for protocol-run episodes, temporal sequence, causal backfill, outcomes, and experiment history.
- Use Lisptc for protocol selection, context composition, tool/API calls, intermediate computation, and reusable procedural memory.
- Use the bridge for protocol compilation, capability membranes, validation, audit, synchronization, and projection builds.
- Never automatically evaluate retrieved/generated prompt content as Lisp.
- Never activate a prompt/protocol revision without immutable source lineage, tests, evaluation, and approval.
